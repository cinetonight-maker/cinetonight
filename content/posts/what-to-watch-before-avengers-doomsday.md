**No, you do not need to watch 35 Marvel films.** If you are working out what to watch before Avengers Doomsday, the real minimum is five titles, about twelve hours, and you can do it comfortably before the film opens on **18 December 2026**.

Ten titles below cover every character and story thread the film actually builds on. Each one is marked **Essential**, **Recommended** or **Optional**, so you can stop wherever your time runs out rather than working through a list that assumes you have a free month. Only have one evening free? There is a five hour version a little further down.

*Last updated 23 August 2026. Release date and cast verified against Marvel's official film page.*

## How we picked these movies

This is not the full Marvel timeline reordered. Every title here had to earn its place against four questions:

1. **Is there a confirmed connection to Doomsday?** Cast announcements and official material, not fan theory.
2. **Does a character from it appear in the film?** Marvel's confirmed cast runs past thirty names. We worked backwards from that list to the films those actors are walking in from.
3. **Does it carry a story thread the film depends on?** The multiverse rules, the Fox-era X-Men, the state of world politics after the last Captain America film.
4. **How much of it does a new viewer genuinely need?** A film that gives useful background but is not required to follow the plot is marked Optional, not quietly left off.

That last question is the one most watch lists skip. It is why this list has ten titles instead of the twenty five you will find elsewhere, and why four of the ten are honestly labelled as skippable.

## Only have one evening? Watch these

Roughly five hours, and it is the highest return you can get from a single sitting:

1. **The Fantastic Four: First Steps**. The team Doom is defined against, arriving directly in this film.
2. **Thunderbolts**. Five confirmed Doomsday cast members, and the film that made them a unit.
3. **The Loki season 2 finale**. One episode, and it is where the multiverse rules the film runs on are actually set.

Two films and one episode. You will miss the emotional weight that Endgame carries, but you will follow every plot beat.

## What to watch before Avengers Doomsday: the full ten, in order

### 1. Avengers: Endgame

**Priority: Essential**

**Why it matters.** Robert Downey Jr returns as Doctor Doom, not as Tony Stark. The entire charge of that casting comes from what Stark meant to these characters and how his story ended. Doomsday is trading on a debt this film created.

**Remember before Doomsday.** Who was in the room when Tony died, and how each of them reacted. Those reactions are what the film will be playing against.

### 2. Loki, seasons 1 and 2

**Priority: Essential**

**Why it matters.** This is the only place Marvel sits down and explains how branching timelines work, what the sacred timeline was, and what replaced it. Doomsday is a multiverse film. Skip this and half its logic arrives unexplained.

**Remember before Doomsday.** Where Loki ends up at the close of season 2, and what he is now holding together. Tom Hiddleston is confirmed for Doomsday and walks in from exactly that position.

### 3. Deadpool and Wolverine

**Priority: Essential**

**Why it matters.** The bridge that carried Fox's mutants into Marvel's main universe, and the film that showed an audience what happens when a reality dies rather than just describing it. Both ideas sit at the centre of Doomsday.

**Remember before Doomsday.** That mutants crossing over is now established, not hypothetical. It is also the lightest watch here, which makes it a good second sitting after Endgame. If that is the tone you want tonight, browse [action](/movies?genre=Action).

### 4. The Fantastic Four: First Steps

**Priority: Essential**

**Why it matters.** Pedro Pascal, Vanessa Kirby, Ebon Moss-Bachrach and Joseph Quinn all move from this film straight into Doomsday. In the source material no hero matters more to Doom than Reed Richards. That rivalry is the oldest in Marvel comics and it is the reason this film exists where it does in the schedule.

**Remember before Doomsday.** The family dynamic, and Reed specifically. If you watch one film from the last two years, make it this. It sits alongside the year's other big [adventure](/movies?genre=Adventure) releases.

### 5. Thunderbolts

**Priority: Essential**

**Why it matters.** The team being marketed as the New Avengers. Florence Pugh as Yelena, Lewis Pullman as Sentry, David Harbour as Red Guardian, Wyatt Russell as US Agent and Hannah John-Kamen as Ghost are all on the confirmed Doomsday cast list.

**Remember before Doomsday.** How they behave as a group rather than what happens to them. That dynamic is what they carry in.

### 6. X-Men: Days of Future Past

**Priority: Recommended**

**Why it matters.** The Sentinels that filled the Doomsday trailer are this film's nightmare made literal. It is also the cleanest explanation of a broken timeline anyone has put on screen, which is the grammar Doomsday is written in.

**Remember before Doomsday.** What the Sentinels are for and who built them. If you watch only one X-Men film, this is it. More in this vein on our [science fiction](/movies?genre=Sci-Fi) shelf.

### 7. X-Men and X2

**Priority: Recommended**

**Why it matters.** Patrick Stewart and Ian McKellen are confirmed for Doomsday. Not new actors in those roles, the originals, twenty five years on. James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn, Alan Cumming and Kelsey Grammer are on the cast list too, which is very nearly that era's roster reassembled.

**Remember before Doomsday.** The Xavier and Magneto relationship as these two actors played it. Everything the reunion is worth depends on remembering that.

### 8. Captain America: Brave New World

**Priority: Recommended**

**Why it matters.** Anthony Mackie's Sam Wilson goes into the biggest fight of his life, with Danny Ramirez alongside him. Just as useful is where the film leaves world governments on the question of enhanced people, which is the political weather Doomsday opens in.

**Remember before Doomsday.** Sam's standing with those governments, not the plot mechanics. Marvel's current theatrical run is tracked on our [latest releases](/latest) page.

### 9. Black Panther: Wakanda Forever

**Priority: Optional**

**Why it matters.** Letitia Wright, Winston Duke and Tenoch Huerta Mejía are all confirmed. Shuri's position, M'Baku's leadership and Wakanda's standing in world politics feed into how the nation appears in Doomsday.

**Remember before Doomsday.** Who leads Wakanda now, and on what terms. Genuinely optional if time is short.

### 10. Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings

**Priority: Optional**

**Why it matters.** Simu Liu is on the cast list, and the ten rings remain one of the largest unexplained objects in the universe.

**Remember before Doomsday.** That the rings signalled something and were never followed up. It is the loosest thread here, which is exactly why it is worth having in mind.

## What you can safely skip

More useful than another list of things to watch:

- **WandaVision and Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.** Good multiverse background, but Loki covers the rules Doomsday actually uses.
- **The Eternals.** No confirmed connection.
- **Most of the Disney+ series.** Only Loki is load-bearing.
- **The wider X-Men run.** X-Men, X2 and Days of Future Past carry the actors and the imagery. The rest is optional.

## A watch plan for the months before December

All ten runs to roughly twenty five hours once both seasons of Loki are counted. The five Essentials are about twelve. Spread across the time remaining that is comfortable:

- **Now through September.** Endgame, then Loki. Get the emotional and multiverse groundwork done while there is no pressure.
- **October.** Deadpool and Wolverine, The Fantastic Four: First Steps, Thunderbolts. The three feeding most directly into the film.
- **November.** Days of Future Past, then Brave New World. Add Wakanda Forever and Shang-Chi if you are enjoying the run.
- **Early December.** X-Men and X2 together. Watching the Fox era as a block makes the returning faces land far harder than watching them months apart.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to watch every Marvel movie before Avengers Doomsday?

No. Thirty five films exist behind Doomsday and you need ten of them at most. Five are genuinely essential: Endgame, Loki, Deadpool and Wolverine, The Fantastic Four: First Steps and Thunderbolts.

### What Marvel movies are actually important before Doomsday?

The five Essentials above. They cover the emotional stakes, the multiverse rules, the mutant crossover, Doom's central rivalry and the New Avengers line-up. Everything else on this list adds depth rather than comprehension.

### Can I watch Avengers Doomsday without watching Loki?

You can follow the plot, but you will be taking the multiverse rules on trust. Loki is the only place Marvel explains how branching timelines work and what replaced the sacred timeline. If you will not watch both seasons, watch the season 2 finale on its own.

### Which X-Men movies should I watch before Doomsday?

X-Men, X2 and Days of Future Past. Those three introduce the exact actors returning in Doomsday, including Patrick Stewart and Ian McKellen, and the Sentinels that appear in the trailer. The later Fox films are not needed.

### How many hours does the Doomsday preparation watch order take?

About twenty five hours for all ten including both seasons of Loki, roughly twelve hours for the five Essentials, and about five hours for the one evening version.

### When does Avengers Doomsday release?

18 December 2026 in cinemas, worldwide including India. Our [release date guide](/blog/avengers-doomsday-release-date-india) tracks booking and showtimes as the date approaches.

### Is Robert Downey Jr playing Doctor Doom?

Yes. He returns as Doctor Doom rather than as Tony Stark, which is exactly why Endgame sits first on this list.
