## Avengers Doomsday release date: what is confirmed

18 December 2026, worldwide, India included. Marvel has generally released its tentpole films day-and-date in India in recent years, and nothing so far suggests this one will differ.

That date falls on a Friday. Indian metro chains often run paid previews the evening before for releases of this scale, and premium-format bookings usually open ahead of the week of release. Neither has been announced for this film.

**What is not confirmed:** booking dates, dub languages, runtime, and anything to do with streaming. Those are covered below and marked as such.

## Avengers Doomsday release date worldwide

**18 December 2026**, and it is a single global date rather than a staggered rollout.

Marvel has released its recent tentpole films on the same day across its major markets, and Doomsday follows that pattern. The United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, most of Europe, India and Pakistan are all expected on the eighteenth, with only the usual local variations in preview screenings and time zones.

That matters for one practical reason: **spoilers travel at the speed of the earliest time zone.** For a film built on cast reveals, the gap between a first showing in Australia and a first showing in North America is roughly a day, so social media is unsafe from the eighteenth onward wherever you are.

Nothing region-specific has been announced beyond the date itself. Booking dates, formats and dub languages come closer to release, market by market.

## The confirmed cast

Marvel's cast list runs past thirty names, the largest the studio has assembled. Grouped by where each is arriving from:

### The Avengers

Chris Hemsworth as Thor, Anthony Mackie as Captain America, Chris Evans, Sebastian Stan, Paul Rudd, Simu Liu, Letitia Wright, Winston Duke, Danny Ramirez and Tenoch Huerta Mejía.

### Loki

Tom Hiddleston, arriving from the end of the second season of his series, which is also the only place the film's multiverse rules are properly explained.

### The Fantastic Four

Pedro Pascal, Vanessa Kirby, Ebon Moss-Bachrach and Joseph Quinn, straight from The Fantastic Four: First Steps. In the comics no hero matters more to Doom than Reed Richards, so this is not incidental casting.

### The Thunderbolts, or New Avengers

Florence Pugh as Yelena, Lewis Pullman as Sentry, David Harbour as Red Guardian, Wyatt Russell as US Agent, Hannah John-Kamen as Ghost and Kathryn Newton.

### The original X-Men

**Patrick Stewart and Ian McKellen** return as Professor X and Magneto, alongside Kelsey Grammer, James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn and Alan Cumming. Channing Tatum is on the list as well.

Not recast. The original actors, twenty five years on from the first X-Men film.

## What the trailer shows

Marvel has released trailer footage, and the details most widely reported from it are Doom appearing in full character with a Latverian accent, and Sentinels, the mutant-hunting machines familiar from Days of Future Past.

Read anything beyond that with care. Trailer analysis is interpretation, not plot confirmation, and Marvel has cut misleading footage before. What is not in dispute is that **Avengers: Secret Wars** follows this film, so Doomsday is setting something up rather than closing it.
