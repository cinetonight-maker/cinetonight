## How to choose in about a minute

A date night movie does not have to be a love story. Three questions get you there faster than any list:

1. **How long have you actually got?** If dinner ran late, a short film beats a great one you will not finish.
2. **Do you want to feel calmer, or more awake?** That single fork removes most of the options.
3. **What can you both actually play tonight?** No point falling for something behind a subscription neither of you has.

## The best date night movies, one by one

### 1. Crazy, Stupid, Love.

**Best for:** romance with plenty of comedy.

Several relationship stories running at once, sharp writing, and one genuine surprise most people do not see coming. Romantic without tipping into sentimental.

**Choose it when** neither of you wants anything serious, but you still want something to talk about.

### 2. About Time

**Best for:** a warm, emotional night.

The romance is central, but the film is really about family and ordinary days. It earns its feeling rather than reaching for it.

**Choose it when** you want warmth rather than flash, and neither of you minds if one of you cries.

### 3. 10 Things I Hate About You

**Best for:** a fun, nostalgic evening.

Still one of the sharpest teen comedies ever written, and it holds up far better than most of its era.

**Choose it when** you want easy laughs with nothing at stake.

### 4. Palm Springs

**Best for:** couples who want something different.

A romantic comedy wrapped around a very strange premise, and funnier for it.

**Choose it when** you are both slightly bored of the genre but not ready to abandon it.

### 5. Before Sunrise

**Best for:** a conversation-focused night.

Two people walking and talking for an evening. Almost nothing happens, which is exactly the point.

**Choose it when** you would rather talk to each other than watch something loud.

### 6. Crazy Rich Asians

**Best for:** a glamorous romantic night.

Big, warm and funny, with a sharper story about family than the marketing suggested.

**Choose it when** you want the evening to feel like an occasion.

### 7. The Big Sick

**Best for:** smart comedy and grounded romance.

Based on a true story, genuinely funny, and honest about the awkward parts most films skip.

**Choose it when** you want to laugh at something that also feels true.

### 8. The Lost City

**Best for:** romance mixed with adventure.

An action comedy that happens to contain a romance, which makes it the easiest sell here.

**Choose it when** one of you does not do romantic films and you need common ground.

### 9. The Princess Bride

**Best for:** a cosy classic.

Adventure, comedy and romance at once, quotable throughout, and it asks nothing of you.

**Choose it when** it is late, you are tired, and you want something that cannot go wrong.

### 10. Knives Out

**Best for:** couples who would rather watch a mystery.

A whodunnit built to be guessed at out loud, with a cast plainly enjoying themselves.

**Choose it when** you both like theorising. More in this vein under [mystery](/movies?genre=Mystery).

### 11. La La Land

**Best for:** romance with something to discuss afterwards.

Gorgeous, melancholy, and the ending splits rooms. That argument is half the value.

**Choose it when** you enjoy disagreeing about films together.

### 12. Rye Lane

**Best for:** a fresh, energetic romantic comedy.

Short, funny and visually alive, and it moves fast enough that nobody reaches for a phone.

**Choose it when** you want the genre done well but not done traditionally.

### 13. The Idea of You

**Best for:** contemporary romance.

Warmer and more grown up than its premise suggests, and it takes its lead seriously.

**Choose it when** you want a straightforward modern romance without irony.

### 14. Anyone But You

**Best for:** the easygoing pick.

Broad, sunny and undemanding. It knows exactly what it is.

**Choose it when** it is Friday, you are both exhausted, and effort is off the table.

### 15. Past Lives

**Best for:** established couples who want something thoughtful.

Quiet, precise and genuinely moving, about the lives you did not end up living.

**Choose it when** you are comfortable enough with each other to sit in silence afterwards.

## What to watch on a first date

A first date movie has one job: give you both things to react to without demanding too much of either of you.

**Safe and strong:** Palm Springs, Knives Out, Crazy, Stupid, Love. and The Lost City. All four are entertaining, easy to follow, and leave natural room to laugh or talk.

**What to avoid:**

- **Anything you love too much.** If you are watching their reaction instead of the film, it is the wrong pick.
- **Heavy emotional drama.** Past Lives is wonderful and completely wrong for a first date.
- **Anything over two and a half hours.** That is a commitment neither of you has agreed to yet.
- **Something one of you has seen and the other has not,** unless the rewatcher genuinely does not mind.

The goal is not proving you have perfect taste. It is picking something you can both enjoy.

## When the two of you want different things

The common date night problem is not choice, it is disagreement. Four situations, and what actually works:

**One of you likes romance, the other does not.** Pick a film with romance inside another genre rather than a romance. The Lost City and Knives Out both do this, and nobody has to compromise out loud.

**One wants to think, the other wants to switch off.** Go with the tired one. A thoughtful film watched by someone half asleep is wasted on you both, and Past Lives will still be there next week.

**You have completely different taste.** Alternate who chooses, and the chooser cannot be vetoed. Taking turns removes the negotiation, and you end up seeing things you would never have picked.

**Neither of you cares enough to decide.** Then whoever cares less should choose, because the one with strong feelings will veto anyway. Or let the [homepage picker](/#tonights-pick) do it, which is quicker than both of you pretending to be easy going.

## Date night does not have to mean a romantic movie

This is the easiest mistake to make. If neither of you particularly enjoys romance, do not force one just because it is date night.

A mystery, an adventure or a thriller works far better if that is what you actually like. The best date night movie is the one you are both still happy to be watching twenty minutes in.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good movie to watch on date night?

One that suits both people and the mood of the evening. Crazy, Stupid, Love. is the strongest all-round pick for romance and comedy together. If you would rather share a mystery, choose Knives Out.

### Does a date night movie have to be romantic?

No. Comedy, thriller, mystery, adventure and drama all work. The only real rule is picking something you will both still be enjoying twenty minutes in.

### What should I watch on a first date?

Light comedy, romantic comedy, mystery or adventure. Palm Springs, The Lost City and Knives Out are entertaining without making the evening intense. Avoid anything over two and a half hours, and anything you personally love too much.

### What are good cosy movies to watch together?

About Time, The Princess Bride, Crazy Rich Asians and 10 Things I Hate About You. All four are warm, easy to settle into, and ask very little of you.

### What if we like completely different films?

Alternate who picks, and do not allow vetoes. Taking turns removes the negotiation and gets you both watching things you would never have chosen alone.

### How can we choose a movie faster?

Decide three things before opening anything: the mood, roughly how long you have, and which services you actually pay for. That turns hundreds of options into three or four.
