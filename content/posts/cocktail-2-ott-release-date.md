## Is it worth your evening?

It is a breezy romance, and that is both the appeal and the limit.

The sequel picks up the spirit of the 2012 original with **Shahid Kapoor, Kriti Sanon and Rashmika Mandanna** leading. The soundtrack and the chemistry between the leads carried the theatrical run, and that combination usually plays better at home than in a cinema, where a lighter film can feel slight.

**Choose it when** you want something warm and undemanding rather than something that asks for your full attention. It is a good fit for a relaxed evening or a shared watch where nobody wants to concentrate hard.

**Skip it tonight if** you want something that will stay with you afterwards. It is not trying to be that film, and judging it as one is unfair to both of you.
